Ark.com was a people search engine founded in San Francisco in March 2012 by Patrick Riley and Yiming Liu, and part of Y Combinator's Winter 2012 batch. It indexed over a billion public social network profiles and let users search across them by hometown, city, school, employer and interests.

The company raised a $4.2M seed round in April 2012 from Andreessen Horowitz, Greylock Partners and SV Angel, and reached the finals of TechCrunch Disrupt NYC 2012.

**Status: defunct.** Y Combinator lists the company as Inactive; PitchBook lists it as Out of Business. As of a July 2026 probe, `ark.com` redirects to `www.ark-invest.com` (the unrelated ARK Investment Management), `https://ark.com` times out, and no `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.` or `www.` subdomain resolves.

Ark never published a public API, developer portal or SDKs, so this repo carries **no API artifacts** — it is retained as a historical a16z portfolio record.

Not to be confused with ARK Investment Management (ark-funds.com), the ARK Ecosystem blockchain (ark.io / ark.dev), or AI Ark (ai-ark.com).
